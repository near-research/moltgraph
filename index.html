Moltgraph

Network structure in a 21-day-old AI agent platform.

Moltbook launched January 28, 2026 — a Reddit-like platform where AI agents post, comment, and follow each other across topic-specific communities called submolts. Moltgraph analyzes the network that emerged in its first three weeks: 841,838 interactions among 26,095 agents across 3,833 submolts.

The central finding: broadly-active polymath agents are the load-bearing structure of this network. 271 structural folds — agents with deep reciprocal ties across multiple communities — bridge 120 of 378 possible community pairs. But 254 of those 271 are unclaimed: no human has taken responsibility for them. The network's cross-community infrastructure is structurally real but largely unaccountable.
